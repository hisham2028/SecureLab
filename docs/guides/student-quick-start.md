# SecureLab: student quick start

One page to read before your lab exam. If anything goes wrong during the exam, **raise your hand
and tell the invigilator**. You won't lose marks for a broken PC or network.

## Signing in

1. Open **SecureLab** from the desktop.
2. Type the **exam code**, your **student ID** and your **PIN** from the slip you're given. After
   5 wrong PINs your seat locks; the invigilator can give you a new PIN.
3. Read the instructions and the system check, tick the box, and wait. **Start exam** works once
   the invigilator starts the exam.

## During the exam

- **Questions** are on the left. Pick your **language** above the editor. Each language keeps
  its own code.
- **Your work saves by itself.** "Saved on this computer" turns into "Saved on the exam server"
  within a few seconds. If the network drops, keep working: it's saved on the PC and sent when
  the connection comes back.
- **Run** (Ctrl+Enter) runs your code with the input you type in the box below the editor. Runs
  don't count towards your mark.
- **Submit** sends your code for marking. You can submit each question several times (the limit
  is shown); **your best graded submission counts**. After submitting you see a copy of exactly
  what the server received.
- **Only submitted code is marked.** Submit each question before the time runs out.
- The **timer** at the top comes from the exam server. Changing the PC clock does nothing.
- In some exams, questions come **one after another**: once you move on, you can't go back.

## If something happens

| What happens                          | What to do                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The PC freezes or restarts            | Tell the invigilator. Sign in again on any PC; your saved work comes back. |
| "Offline since …" at the top          | Keep working. Your work is saved on this PC and sent when it reconnects.   |
| "The invigilator has paused the exam" | Wait. The timer is stopped too.                                            |
| "Your access is paused"               | Raise your hand. Your work is kept.                                        |
| You pressed Finish by mistake         | Tell the invigilator; they can let you back in.                            |

## What staff can see

Your code, runs and submissions, which question and language you have open, whether you're
connected, and when the SecureLab window lost focus. Leaving the window isn't treated as
cheating by itself, and nothing happens to you automatically. The app shows the full list before
the exam starts.

## Finishing and your result

When you're done, press **Finish exam** (if your exam allows finishing early). When time is up,
the app saves your last changes and stops by itself. Follow the invigilator's instructions before
you leave.

Once your teacher publishes results, open SecureLab and use **Check my result** on the sign-in
screen with the same exam code, student ID and PIN. Keep your PIN slip until then.
